AAC Block Study

College project on the properties, application, and practical use of AAC blocks in modern construction.

Category
Materials

A college project focused on the properties, application, and practical use of Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) blocks in modern construction.

Key findings

  • Compared AAC block density, thermal performance, and fire resistance with conventional brick.
  • Documented cost and productivity implications for low-rise and mid-rise construction.
  • Presented recommendations for sustainable and efficient walling systems in local context.
