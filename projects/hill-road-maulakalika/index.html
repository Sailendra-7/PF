Detail Survey and Design of Hill Road (Maulakalika Temple Route)

Maulakalika Temple, NepalJanuary 2025

Detailed survey and road design from the base of Maulakalika Temple to the temple area, including route planning and geometric design.

A detailed survey and road design project covering the route from the base of Maulakalika Temple to the temple area, including route planning and geometric design considerations.

Key findings

  • Performed topographic data collection using Total Station for hill alignment planning.
  • Developed horizontal and vertical geometry considering safety, slope, and terrain constraints.
  • Prepared design outputs and route documentation for practical field implementation.

Tools & methods

  • Surveying (Total Station)
  • Road Design (SW Road)
  • AutoCAD
